# Luxton Technique

P.H. Lee was triggered by the [[X-Card|X-Card]] and [[Lines-and-Veils|Lines and Veils]] as their solutions to triggering content is to remove and ignore or muddle the subject, then continue. The concept of pretending something never happened was itself a trigger, and prompted anxiety related to denial and dismissal when part of the environment.

Once triggered, the traumatic experience happens, the focus must be on the interpretation of the event as either a damaging(reinforcement of PTSD) or healing(helps recontextualize the experience) experience.

"I believe that, in principle, good techniques for dealing with PTSD in role-playing games will avoid damaging experiences and center healing experiences."

Named after the GM at AmberCon NW

Before Play: Supportively discuss potential triggers, with the understanding that not everything can be fully accounted for, and without pressure to disclose trauma
During Play: Triggered players can bring up discussion and have control over how the subject is handled
