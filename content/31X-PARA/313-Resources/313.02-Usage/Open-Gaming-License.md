# Open-Gaming-License

The 1.0a OGL was made by WotC for DnD 3rd edition in 2000 to allow third-publishers to produce compatible materials (a la Microsoft). The license was used in large by the industry as the most well known and open legal document allowing for third party products.

It was intended to be unmodifiable and irrevocable however, in 2022 WotC attempted to push a version 1.1 which would deauthorize the 1.0a. Following mass protest, they attempted to push a version 1.2 which addressed little. Finally, they released SRD 5.1 under the [[Creative-Commons.CC-BY#4.0]].

This directly lead to the creation of the [[Open-RPG-Creative-License|ORC License]] and the historically most profitable year for many other publishers.
