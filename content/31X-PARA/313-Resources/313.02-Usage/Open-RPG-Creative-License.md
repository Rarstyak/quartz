# ORC

The Open RPG Creative License(ORC) was produced by Azora Law with the input of hundreds of RPG publishers, lead by [[Paizo]] after WotC attempted to negate the [[Open-Gaming-License|OGL(v1.0a)]] with an OGL(v1.1). It is dedicated to the public domain and registered in the Library of Congress under TX 9-307-067.
