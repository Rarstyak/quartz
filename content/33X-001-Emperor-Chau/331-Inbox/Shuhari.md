# Shuhari

1. A time to thoroughly imitate the master (a time to protect [[#shu]])
2. A time to study other styles (a time to break them [[#ha]])
3. A time to pursue your own research (a time to leave [[#ri]])

## Shu

People whose drawings are not recognized by other are at a stage where their own sense of art does not match the standards of those around them

You may have outdated aesthetics

Tuning: find the overlap between your own value standard and the world's value standard

There is often a sense of discomfort from denying one's personal feelings; but between yourself and a widely recognized master, who is more in line with the values of those around you?

You need to imitate the parts you like and the parts you dislike about their style, the part that feels off, is likely where you are lacking and need to grow

## Ha

Find common ground among other styles

This is where you find universal themes/values

## Ri

You wont get far in trying to develop something new, if you haven't found the universal themes/values
