# Philharmonic Vampyres

The Vamps are a prankster gang who were dubbed the "Philharmonic Vampyres" a few decades ago when five members clad in tuxedos, capes, and vampire teeth flew up on wires behind the Night City Symphony on Halloween night. Are they artists, hoodlums, or social com mentators? Are those teeth part of the costume or not? Who knows?

The Philharmonic Vampyres weren’t always steeped in goth culture. Originally, they were a prankster gang. The moniker comes from a prank pulled in the late 2010s, when five members of the gang photobombed the Night City Symphony by dressing up in tuxedos, capes, and vampire fangs while flying behind the orchestra on wires during a Halloween concert. Over time, the gang evolved. Members increasingly leaned into the "vampyre" theme until the dark artistic styling became more important than the pranks. Today, the gang is split in two. The most visible faction, led by The Master, are full-on goth and focus primarily on horror-themed artistic presentations at the Night City Symphony Hall. A smaller faction, led by an NCU student known as Acme, wants to return to the days of grand pranks. Despite the split, most members of the two factions get along. The pranksters enjoy the parties thrown by the goths, and many of the goths join in on the pranking, seeing it as a thrilling form of "artistic expression."

The Philharmonic Vampyres are more likely to flee than fight, but when cornered, a surprising number prove adept at martial arts skills.
