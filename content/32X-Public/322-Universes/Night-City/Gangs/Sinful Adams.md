# The Sinful Adams

What’s the collective noun for a group of goths? A coven? A crypt? A misery? A murder? Whatever it is, that’s the Sinful Adams. Many consider the Adams to be a posergang, since members often bodysculpt themselves to resemble goth icons of the past, but that isn’t a requirement for membership. The gang claims no ownership of the concept of "goth" and welcomes adherents from the style’s many subcultures.

Surprisingly civic-minded, the Sinful Adams have set up shop in an old graveyard, now known as the Boneyard. There, they’ve built a shanty town to offer refuge to a small homeless community. The Adams grow food to share with their charges, brew their own booze out of berries, and even run a small school. Every Halloween, the gang organizes a carnival, open to residents from the surrounding area.

The gang’s war leader, Ophelia, ensures each member is trained in combat. Pistols, long blades, cyberweapons, and martial arts are among the most favored options.

## Locations

\#1 The Boneyard
Security Code 10: The Sinful Adams, Hardened Light

The Boneyard touches on The Glen and the University District, but most of it is located firmly in South Night City. It's also near the Hot Zone, meaning no one is itching to pave it over in order to build a parking lot or a monument to some rich corpzoner fluffing themselves.

This shanty town is built on the grounds of the old Night City Garden of Rest. As refugees were forced out of other districts, they needed homes and communities, so they made them here. After all, the dead don’t need the land above their graves, right? The place is oddly peaceful, though some residents claim to hear voices near the still-remaining headstones. Speaking of the dead, the soil is unbelievably lush and can grow nearly anything with a little effort. I’m not suggesting that the fertility of the soil comes from the decaying bodies buried under it … but I’m not denying it, either.

The Boneyard is Sinful Adams' turf, though a small cadre of Tombstone Preservers also hang out there. The Sinful Adams are a local neo-goth gang that protects the shanty town and its residents. They take significant exception to anyone desecrating the graves or homes. The gang’s war leader, Ophelia (Solo 5), acts as the community peacekeeper while the boss, Gomez (Rockerboy 4), runs Poe’s Respite, a three-room schoolhouse set up to educate local kids. Of course, no community is complete without a local bar. The Crypt is open most nights and sells a gin-based drink made with locally grown mixed berries, lemon juice, and a splash of club soda. It's called the Monster Mash, and you can buy it for 10eb (Cheap) as long as the locals like you.
