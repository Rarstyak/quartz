# 20261011 Ep 27 Red Chrome Cargo

Red Chrome hijacks the Hammerhead, a heavily armored Militech cargo train.

She knows the Taniwha hate both the Red Chrome Legion and Iron Sights, both are plaguing Old Japantown, and both are fighting over some shipment. She doesn't care what they are squabbling over as long as neither party nor Militech gets it. REWARD 1000eb in cash or in Sanroo limited edition merch.
