### Description

**Lore**

Bellatrix "Blue Star" Bayer is a 26-year-old, 162 cm tall female edgerunner of Night City and a street-level boss of the Upper Marina gang, the Sons of Taniwha. She was raised in the gang from a young age by her Greek father, a cybernetics tech, and her Japanese mother, ex-Tyger Claws; along with her older brother Rigel "Bright Star", fraternal twin brother Betelgeuse "Burning Star", and younger brother Alnilam "Little Star".

She is the leader of an idoru group, the Vavalo (oracle) Vixens. It is made up of Ilamutu (eldest daughters) in the Sons of Taniwha. They perform song covers, dances, and narrate dramatic readings of traditional myths with modern Night City morals.

While their parents had long passed on, her twin brother Burning Star was killed by the Red Chrome Legionaires shortly before the campaign start. After normally presiding over his funeral, she installed his Internal Linear Sigma Frame and impersonated him for a full straight week in a fit of cyberpsychosis. Found to be shockingly competent at killing Red Chrome, she has demanded and been awkwardly promoted as a stand-in for her brother, posthumously awarded accomplishments in revenge and the rank of street boss.

Wields a Xiphos Sword and Eagletech Survivalist Crossbow/Rifle. Has way too many Cyberaudio Suite, Smart Lens, and Smart Glasses options. Signature move: waltzing through a smoke grenade and chopping everyone up.

### Character Permissions

Blue Star is a fictional half-Greek, half-Japanese woman living in Night City 2045 of a Cyberpunk RED TTRPG game. She was raised in and is now a ranking member of a primarily Samoan gang.

Her metallic dark bronze tattoos are inspired by (and are extremely simplified versions of) Samoan tattoos for women. They denote her life story, her standing with the Aualuma (Women's Association), her position as an Ilamutu (eldest daughter), and her rank within the gang. **When simplified, the tattoos should not be changed in pattern or placement.**

**The Malu (Women), Fa Gogo (Seagull footprint), Vaetuli (Tuli footprint), Jellyfish, Flower, and Star motifs have real-life spiritual meaning to the Samoan people and are not indicators of gang affiliation. They are also traditionally black and not readily visible in casual wear.**

**Blue Star is a fictional gangster and is inspired by real-life motifs, but is not meant to comment on or caricature real-life people or cultures.** If you have any good sources for learning about the Samoan culture, please let me know, as it has been very difficult to research.
