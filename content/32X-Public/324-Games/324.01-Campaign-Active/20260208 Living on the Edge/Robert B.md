# Robert B

**Robert B** (Solo 4): The head bartender at the Jesse James Kosher Deli. Robert's quick wit and quicker fists help keep Brick’s followers in line when the City Manager is out of the office. He knows everyone in his faction and has a weakness for redheads of all genders.
