# Sengo

[[20260913 Ep 23 Transporter Drone Down]]

This Iron Sight-controlled business specializes in blades: both the type one wields and the type one implants. The manager, **Sengo** ([Tech 4](https://app.demiplane.com/nexus/cyberpunkred/roles/tech)), brags about the training he received from a weaponsmith deep in the mountains of Japan, but I happen to know he is a former employee of Great Lakes Cutlery who was fired for stealing company supplies.
