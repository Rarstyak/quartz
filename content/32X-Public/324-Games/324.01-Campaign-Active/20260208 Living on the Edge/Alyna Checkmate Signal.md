# Alyna

![](https://i.imgur.com/AYMJm2G.png)

- 🌐 Demographics
  - Full Name: Alyna
  - Pronouns: She/Her
  - 🎲 [[32X-Public/321-Systems/Cyberpunk-RED/Cyberpunk-RED.md|Cyberpunk-RED]] Tech 4, Medtech 4
- 💖 Relationships
  - 💖 Lover [[Lacey Davis|Lacey Love]]

Her family is a group of Nomadic Mercenaries that venture from Combat Zone to Combat Zone doing various work. But one day a nomad group entered Night City and drove into the CEO of Arasaka's Child critically injuring the child before driving away, creating a conspiracy that it was Alyina's Family that did that act since they were neutral mercenaries that bounced around a lot and didn't take sides which meant that they were never 100% controllable.

So, as a last effort, her family split apart to try and stay low. Alyna doesn't know where the rest of her family went, but she found initial refuge with a boostergang. She spend time there refining her skills and in her teenage years, she experimented with love and tech which caused two of her four past lovers to go cyberpsycho and whom of which were part of that boostergang. The last of which was a foursome with Aurora (Heat), and two Boostergangers, one of which turned cyberpsycho and had to be killed, while the other boosterganger who trusted her was humiliated by his lack of critical thought and forced Alyna to escape the gang.

Now on the run, she ran into the gang ran by Lord Affa where at the time she joined his gang as a Tech with all inventions rights being given to Affa in return for a closely monitored and rent free place of residence. The rent's cost was only free because she agreed to let them monitor all audio that she hears and encounters at all times via a mandatory Cyberaudio Suite, Audio Recorder, and Internal Agent.

From there she lived for a number of years, waiting for the dust to settle with Arasaka's Conspiracy while on occasions going out for Gigs and enjoying some friendship with Aurora (whom are now former lovers with potential to get back together) and Breaker who was a shooting buddy.
