# Vavalo Vixens

![](https://i.imgur.com/QfNPbvQ.png)

Comprised of [[Moku Kea|The White Ship]] Aualuma's top dancers and vocalists, the Sukeban Idoru group holds regular concerts in the community spaces on the White Ship, venues considered "Large Important Clubs" whenever the bosses need meeting entertainment that aren't third party spies, and publish song covers and dramatic readings on the Data Pool.

Mascot branding is a kitsune holding a mere in its mouth jumping out of a kava bowl.

They use Blue Star's room as a clubroom and recording studio.

Blue Star is mostly a voice actor, who publishes dramatic readings, and ASMR on the net.
