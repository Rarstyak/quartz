# Cassandra Bredon

Cassandra has a great talent 16 in 'Contortionist'.
She has an 8 in Music Harmonica.

She eats off the crust before turning the rest into a calzone.

Carlos gives her a custom Harmonica and Fresh Food pizza.

Cassandra, much like Carlos is an easy going person with dreams of leaving the conflict of the Nomad life behind. She thinks about the world more deeply then Carlos does, and wants to make something of herself. She is not completely content with the way her and Carlos' situation has turned out as much of it was his fault. Having accidentally killing her cousin when they found the two together out in the wasteland. Since that day, the leader of her nomad pack, The Pit Vipers, has negotiated an alliance with most of the other packs to target the Steel Vaqueros until they surrender Carlos to them or till the Vaqueros are destroyed.

Cassandra is kind and outspoken, loves animals and has an interest in environmentalism. Being a nomad she works almost exclusively in cargo transportation and rarely visit's Night City. This has put a strain on her and Carlos' relationship, but Carlos is ever dutiful in trying to make the most of the time they have together. Cassandra has beef with Biotechnica and their exotic animal and pet trade. There has been a few times that she has taken part in protests and corporate sabotage against the mega corp, which has also put a target on her back. It has also been a cause of friction between her and Carlos as he would rather they keep their heads down with how much trouble they're in already. Her interests also include music, gardening, hunting, going to clubs, dancing, and going to church. **She is of Latin decent with dark skin, green eyes, long dark brown hair, and has some tattoo's.**
