# Sina Tuigamala

Sina isn't particularly malicious, in fact she can be quite kind, when she is aware of you at all.

Only daughter of one of the five female Matai, Matai Tuigamala, Sina is used to getting whatever she desires... even if she never explicitly states it.

Pampered Princes Samoan Mother, Unknown Father Unbelievably clear skin, smooth hair, a pampered princess living in a castle of clouds. Indifferent to all but what interests her, and only then it is fleeting. The heaviest thing she has lifted in her life is her ego, the second heaviest is her pet Datarabbit.

- Samoan Light Tattoos (Malu NOT Pe'a!)
- Toxin Binders
- Nasal Filters
- Fashionware (Chemskin, Techhair, Glow Nails, Shift Tacts)
- Top 25 in the whole Upper Marina at Composition, Painting/Drawing/Sculpting

There is no shortage of her adoring fans, and sycophant followers who work to create the perfect environment they believe her mother would appreciate.

The worst thing about Sina, is that she doesn't care to use the power her influence affords, nor does she make any effort to monitor any of her 'friends' who might abuse it in her name.
