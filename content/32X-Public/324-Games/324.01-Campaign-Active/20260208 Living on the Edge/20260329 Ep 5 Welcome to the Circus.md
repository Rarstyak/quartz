# 20260329 Ep 5 Welcome to the Circus

![](https://i.imgur.com/eRoud8h.gif)

[[Sagalala Leota]] wants us to acquire an item from a corporate contact.

Breaker driving 5 others in a Compact Ground Car.

We meet with 3 [[Arasaka]] contacts in a pawn shop who try and fail to intimidate us into paying more.

Bozos arrive at the pawn shop in a van and a muscle car. Breaker unloads his flamethrower in the back room. Lacey chops a Bozo in half. The Bozos bomb down the shop with a molotov cocktail as John arrives.

Blue Star convinces the Arasaka mooks to join the fight (and the guy behind the desk [[Yuto Nakamura]] becomes a fan).

With the dealers, we obliterate the [[Bozos]]. The pawnshop owner crawls out with a fire extinguisher.
