# Plush of Taniwha

[[Tarako]], Lacey's Sapient Plushie Dragon
[[Cotija]], Carlos' Sapient Plushie Ox
[[Pez]] Cassandra's Sapient Plushie Capybara
[[Eloise Skull Crusher Davis]], Elys' Sapient Plushie Jaguar
[[Charlotte Davis]],(Charlie) Elys' Sapient Plushie Brown Bear
[[Mixcoatl]], Blue Star's Sapient Plushie Coatl
[[Coatlicue]], Blue Star's Sapient Plushie Snake

All of the sapient plushies are fans of [[Bellatrix Blue Star Bayer|Blue Star]], and help report any suspicious activities while helping care for the YoGangers of the Sons of Taniwha. They often appear in [[Vavalo Vixens]] music videos.

They have no skills, but can fit 1-2 subdermal pockets worth of items depending on size. No impact on concealability, just an additional option for what that concealment looks like (a sapient plushie).
