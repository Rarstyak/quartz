# 20260927 Ep 25 A Beautiful Day for an NCART Heist

The crew is on their way back home from an NCART exchange, when the train they're on is hijacked.

CART 1, was just us.

CART 2 has corpos who need signatures to keep their insurance. Everyone eventually signs, but Carlos is an asshole, gets grabbed, his gun taken, and emptied of bullets. The corpos are fans of [[Bellatrix Blue Star Bayer|Blue Star]].

CART 3 has a rave going on, Blue Star dances them into a less chaotic pattern and drops a gas mask for Crystal. Carlos trips and ends up high on Blue Glass for a bit.

CART 4 has bozos robbing civilians. Civilians who also love [[Bellatrix Blue Star Bayer|Blue Star]]. A pink haired gal from cart 4 joins us.

CART 5 has 3 bodyguards beating up their corpo boss? They tell us to move on.

CART 6 has a bunch of sentient stuffed animals, who are all [[Bellatrix Blue Star Bayer|Blue Star]]'s fans. Blue Star wraps Snake and Quetzalcoatl plushies around her waist.

The Front Cart has two [[Bozos#The Fool]], [[Bozos#Dunce]] [[Bozos#Burt the Squirt]], [[Bozos#Jester]]. Crystal sprints to the front to face off with Jester at the controls. Pink hair pops Dunce with Lacey. Blue Star goes after Burt who has the acid paint Hentai Gun. Blue Star gets grabbed and pulled back. Crystal continues chopping heads off. Carlos shoots off Jester's right cyberleg. Elys and Pink Hair crit the Bozo grabbing Blue Star.

We kill all the Bozos except Jester, who jumps out the window and flies away.
