# Sons of Taniwha

The Sons of Taniwha are something of a rising star in the underworld of Night City. Affa himself is a business man, he makes deals, acquires debts, and pushes pawns where they need to be. He has substantial stakes in product transport and shipping in and out of the Upper Marina. Many people benefit from the Son's work as couriers and transport security.

Lord Affa however, is demanding and as of late his ambitions have only grown. Lately his interests have expanded to not only maintaining his mercantile endeavors, but also carve out territory from Little China as well as expanding his business to the outer city. You all for the most part work as enforcers, how deep your loyalties lie is up to you as well as the nuances of your work. People from all walks of life have joined the Son's of Affa, either directly or indirectly. Some have joined for protection, some in pursuit of power, and some looking to relieve financial burdens. However, all come with the weight of Affa's desire for control.

As part of the gang you are given housing at a reduced average rate should you choose to receive it, as well as protection from rival gangs and outside cooperate groups. This extends to direct family ties, but said family do not need to know about your involvement with the Sons. Affa's headquarters is a large bath house run by him. **Traditional Traditional**

The traditional traditional va has a lot to do with acknowledgement/respect. The explaining of your lineage and societal role and demeaning of oneself to present mana and tapu to the hosts/elders. The myths put humans as the last of the genealogical family of Tagaloa(god), the youngest siblings of humans, animals, plants, the cosmos, sea, and land. The language reflects that man is a part of, not the overseer or manager of the land (eleele means blood and also earth/dirt; palapala means blood and also mud; fanua means land and also the placenta of a woman). It's all about respecting your elders and acknowledging that apart from that all things are equal.

There's also the distinction between the original non-human gods Atua who live in Pulota or Lagi (otherworld or heaven) and ancestral spirits Aitu of human origin. The Aitu were represented as animals, birds, plants, other natural objects. After death, becoming an intermediate between humans and the rest of creation, bridging the gap and creating a va (sacred relationship) between the living Samoan people and the natural world.

Tapu is mostly concerned with paying homage and respect to resource use and family structure (we are all family) because if you don't, there are negative spiritual repercussions. Taniwha leadership might be breaking every rule in the book, but if you break tapu in regards to them, that is still a mark on your soul because you are failing your obligations.

**Western Contact**

European/Western Judeo-Christian perspectives towards nature in the universal singular god in heaven > human > land overturned a LOT. The Samoans burned and ate representatives of their Aitu, converted practically 100% to Christianity, and adopted to land ownership and management. Industrialization and productivity to show piety via tithes and donations to the church has created competing goals within the church, which pulls away from environmental stewardship. How to reconcile both Christianity and traditional values and attitudes towards the environment while respecting both as equally important without demeaning either is a cultural discussion today.
