# Candiss

©@nd!\$\$ (Rockerboy 6, Rep 5) is a 21 year old XBD star with bigger boobs than Lacey. She is a Black/White American Demonique biosculpt from Neo Orleans who is famous for rapping with her mouth full and many of her XBD's involve diss tracks against other XBD artists.

Unlike many (though mostly the male) XBD stars, she does not have a braindance recorder herself. Her crew gets the fans of other XBD stars to sign off on braindance installation and a recorded sweepstakes. They go about their days and either have weird stuff happen to them as extras in the final XBD and/or get surprised by Candiss herself, often while admiring the subject of the diss track.

She is famous for stealing higher profile clients in this way. Lacey is far from the only XBD star who hates her.

In [[20260913 Ep 23 Transporter Drone Down]], the crew noticed a graffiti artist tagging a wall in the Hot Zone with "Lacey Love lays like a dead fish." This was the chorus for Candiss' hottest release last month featuring Mister Studd (Rockerboy 4, Rep 5), who she describes as sufficient.
