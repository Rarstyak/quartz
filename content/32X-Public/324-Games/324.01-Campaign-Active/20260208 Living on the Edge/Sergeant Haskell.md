# Sergeant Haskell

Viktor "Breaker" Petrov grew up on city streets that eventually became a Combat Zone. He joined Militech young and excelled, but poor intel and internal politics sent his team on a doomed mission. Only he and his sergeant survived, and together they decided enough was enough, and deserted. Sgt. Haskell already had contacts in the city and was quickly able to set himself up as a small time fixer, feeding Breaker jobs now and again. But not enough to make a living. When you're on the run and trying to steer clear of the corps, the gangs quickly become your only option. It was just a little loan, the first time... now they basically own him, and he's not happy about it.
