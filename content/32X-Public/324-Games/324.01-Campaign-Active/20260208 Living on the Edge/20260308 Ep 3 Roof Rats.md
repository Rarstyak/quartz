# 20260308 Ep 3 Roof Rats

![](https://i.imgur.com/Z3eK3FA.gif)

A drug shipment has been stolen from the docks of the Sons of Taniwha.

We learn that the theft was done by a group calling themselves the Roof Rats, 5-10.

We notice a lookout at a trash fire.

We learn about a dive bar they like to visit. Lacey and Carlos go to check in for information. They pose as city strutters, slumming it. They leave the bar with two Roof Rats following to mug them.

We see a free clinic. Blue Star escorts Elys D and Crystal as potential volunteer MedTechs. They find a folder, likely containing the info on the drug donors.

The [[Roof Rats]] pull knives, Blue Star goes into work mode and sniper fire fills the alley. Carlos kicks in a man's chest.
