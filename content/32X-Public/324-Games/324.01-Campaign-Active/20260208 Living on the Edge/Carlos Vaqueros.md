Romeo and Juliet with member from which rival Nomad Pack?

The Steel Vaqueros and Sons of Taniwha have some dealings, and as part of it, Carlos and a few others are 'on loan' to Lord Affa.

Carlos began his life born to one of the many family units within the Steel Vaqueros towards the end of the 2020s. He spent his years traveling with his pack and becoming adept with the trades of his people. As the world continued to heal from the devastation of the 4th Corporate War he and his brother Marcus became acquainted with Night City. While Carlos busied himself with the much needed trade of the city's outskirts, his brother aliened himself with the ganger culture of the city center.

Marcus joined a successful street gang in the hopes that he could help provide for his family, however this connection to the gang came at a heavy price. To continue keeping up with his fellow gangers he became more and more chromed out and delving deep into the combat zone. This made him distant to his family and made him almost unrecognizable. Tragedy would strike when one night Carlos found his brother slumped over in his bed seemingly mumbling to himself. When Carlos went to speak to his brother, Marcus attacked him lashing out with his mantis blades. Carlos being unaugmented as he was stood no chance against his brother and realized to his horror that Marcus had fallen into cyber-psychosis. The young nomad tried desperately to reason with his afflicted brother as he attacked him, but his reasoning was cut short when Marcus severed  Carlos' legs from the knees down. Had it not been for the Vaqueros pack enforcers taking out Marcus, Carlos would not have survived.

Now Carlos struggles with the loss of his brother and the current political struggles of his pack. For generations the Steel Vaqueros' dominance over the coastal highway has left many other nomad packs struggling for work. One of these packs happened to be home to Carlos' lover [[Cassandra Bredon]]. Her association with the Vaqueros' rivals has left her at threat from Carlos' own pack. Now Carlos must deal with the challenges posed by the rivals of the Vaqueros and the threat his pack poses his lover.
